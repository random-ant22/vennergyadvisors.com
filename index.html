Where We Work

Three forces shaping the grid — one point of intersection.

Energy strategy today can't be built in a silo. We work across the three domains reshaping the grid, so our clients make decisions that hold up across all of them.

T

Transmission

Development, siting, and permitting strategy — including transmission rate applications, service agreements, and FERC policy, rulemaking, and compliance.

G

Generation

Interconnection queue management, land rights, and power purchase and supply contract negotiation for projects bringing new capacity online.

L

Large Load Development

Guidance for data centers and other large loads on bring-your-own-power rules, land rights, microgrids, and co-locating with generation.

One Grid, Three Pressures

The infrastructure is converging. Your strategy should too.

New generation is racing to interconnect. Large loads are reshaping siting decisions overnight. Transmission is the constraint tying it all together. Vennergy Advisors works at that convergence point every day.
