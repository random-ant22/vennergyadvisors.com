About Vennergy Advisors

Strategy built for where energy actually happens — at the intersection.

Vennergy Advisors was founded on a simple observation: the hardest problems in energy don't live neatly inside transmission, generation, or load. They live in the overlap.

Our approach

The grid is being reshaped by new generation coming online, transmission struggling to keep pace, and large loads — from data centers to industrial facilities — competing for capacity and timelines that used to be measured in years, now compressed into months.

Vennergy Advisors works across all three domains at once. We provide consulting and fractional executive services to developers, utilities, large energy users, and policymakers who need strategy that holds up no matter which side of the table they're sitting on.

That means we don't just advise on a single transaction or a single policy fight. We help clients see how their decisions on siting, contracts, interconnection, and regulatory strategy connect — and we bring the relationships and hands-on experience to move projects and policy forward.

What guides our work

Cross-domain fluency

We speak the language of transmission planners, generation developers, and large load procurement teams — and translate between them.

Practical, transactional experience

Our advice is grounded in the realities of siting, permitting, interconnection, and contract negotiation — not just policy theory.

Outcomes over positions

Whether we're advocating for affordability policy or mediating a dispute, we work toward resolutions that let projects keep moving.
